## Missed facts
- The account's own ledger settles the free-plan question better than any blog: one grant 'Registration Credits +10' on 2026-11-11, nothing since (14 days), so there are no daily credits; and two Seedance 1.5 Pro video attempts that day (2.4 credits each) were refunded about 2 seconds after being charged. Video on the free plan did not go through; the reason is not recorded. (Higgsfield connector, transactions, 2026-11-25)
- The promo window is short and its end is ambiguous: the live sale entry 'sale_11_11', phase 'black_friday_phase_2', runs 2026-11-25T00:00Z to 2026-11-26T00:00Z, i.e. it ends at 01:00 Paris time on Nov 26. The label 'phase 2' suggests another phase follows, so prices after tonight are unknown (could be the same, better or list price: Plus 49 EUR, Ultra 129 EUR, excl. VAT).
- Trial details the researcher did not state: (a) per the connector's own documentation, saying 'cancel auto-renewal' stops the upcoming charge 'while keeping trial access', so the renewal can be cancelled right after starting instead of on day 3 (documented behaviour, not tested); (b) each unlimited request is capped to 1 result (count is forced to 1); (c) the trial gives 0 credits, so anything outside the unlimited list (Seedance 2.0 Mini, Kling, Wan, Veo, Higgsfield Intone, Sync Lipsync, upscalers) would only have the 10 existing credits; (d) the connector's static description still mentions a different variant ('3-day $0 Plus trial with MCP-only credits'), so the offer can differ by account or date. Today this account is offered the Ultra unlimited kind.
- Voice and lip-sync costs are missing from the budget. Live quote: one French text-to-speech line of about 110 characters on Text to Speech V2 (ElevenLabs engine) = 0.45 credits (quoted with a placeholder voice id), so about 12 lines cost roughly 5 credits. The catalog also has Sync Lipsync 3 (re-syncs the lips of an existing clip to an audio track), Higgsfield Intone (1-2 characters speaking 1-6 voice recordings, 2-30 s of voice, output 4-30 s, 480p-1080p, 4:3 available) and Qwen Audio 3.0 TTS with an explicit 'fr' language option; Intone and Sync Lipsync prices could not be quoted without uploaded media.
- Voice consistency: Seedance 2.0 / Mini accept up to 3 audio reference clips (2-15 s each, needs at least one image or video reference) and Seedance 2.5 up to 10. Feeding the same recorded or synthesized French voice lines as references is the catalog's supported way to keep each character's voice identical from shot to shot; relying on native audio alone gives no such guarantee. How well this works in French is untested.
- French speech was not verified for any model in this budget answer. From memory only (training data before mid-2026, not checked today): Kling's native audio launched with a short language list (Chinese and English on 2.6, a few more on 3.0) in which I do not recall French, while Veo 3.x does speak French. If that still holds, 'Kling 3.0 with sound' is a risky pick for French dialogue. A French test clip must come before any purchase.
- Format fit on Higgsfield: only Seedance (2.0, Mini, 2.5, 2.5 Flash), Wan 3.0, Grok Imagine Video 1.5 Lite, MAGI-2 and FLUX 3 output 4:3 natively; Kling 2.6 / 3.0 / 4.0 and Veo do not (crop needed).
- Cheaper mid-quality options sit in the same live quotes but were not used in route 3: Wan 3.0 at 720p with native audio = 7.5 credits per 5 s (4:3, start image, audio references), Seedance 2.0 Fast at 480p = 5 credits per 5 s. With Fast 480p a 30 s sketch is 16-30 x 5 = 80-150 credits plus stills.
- Cost follows seconds generated, not shot count: Seedance 2.0 Mini bills 0.5 credit/s at 480p up to 15 s per clip (15 s = 7.5 credits, live quote), minimum 4 s (2 credits). Longer shots holding two or three lines cost the same per second and mean fewer cuts to keep consistent.
- Nano Banana Pro costs the same 2 credits at 2K as at 1K (live quote), so 1K stills save nothing.
- A 'Basic/Starter' tier is real in Higgsfield's plan logic (model metadata: Seedance 2.0 mode 'fast' is 'available from the Basic/Starter plan'), but its price can only be read on the website pricing page; whether it includes Seedance 2.0 Mini is not stated anywhere I could reach.

## Notes
Limits of this check, stated plainly: I could not do any web research. The run's web search budget (200 calls shared by all agents) was already used up before my first query, and direct page fetches failed with DNS errors for higgsfield.ai, support.google.com and help.openai.com (hosts outside this session's allowed network; they would have to be allowed in the session's network settings). So every claim about third-party services (8 to 13: Google Flow, Dreamina, Kling, Grok, Sora dates, Meta, Hailuo, PixVerse, Vidu, Wan) is marked uncertain, not because I found contrary evidence but because nothing could be checked. Nothing in them was refuted.

What I did verify, with my own calls to the live Higgsfield connector at about 06:12 UTC on 2026-11-25 (read-only: balance, transactions, pricing config, model catalog, cost quotes; nothing generated, nothing bought, no trial started): all Higgsfield plan prices, the trial terms, the unlimited perks, the top-up config, 36 video quotes and 12 image quotes. Every number matched the researcher's. This is an independent re-query of the same official system, not a second independent source.

Net effect on the recommendation: the Higgsfield side is solid. Seedance 2.0 Mini at 480p is indeed the cheapest model with native audio in the catalog (2.5 credits per 5 s), 4:3 native, with start image, reference images and audio references. The three things the researcher asks to confirm before paying remain open (Starter price and contents, whether the 10 free credits can run any video, free allowances for a French account on Flow and Dreamina); on the second one the account ledger points to 'no'. Two additions matter for the decision: French speech quality is untested on every candidate model, and the promo prices are flagged to end at 01:00 Paris time on Nov 26 with the next phase unknown. The 3-day trial stays the only zero-cost way to test French dialogue on Seedance 2.0 / 2.5 before spending; it needs the user's card and his own decision.
