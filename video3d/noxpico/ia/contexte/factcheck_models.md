## Missed facts
- Fact-check limits: web search was unavailable (the shared 200-search budget for this turn was already used up) and page fetches are blocked by this session's network allowlist (en.wikipedia.org, platform.openai.com, huggingface.co and docs.x.ai all failed and would have to be allowed in the session's network settings). Every verdict marked confirmed rests on my own re-read of the live Higgsfield catalogue, cost quotes and pricing; every web-only fact (speech-language lists, arena ranks, vendor API prices, Dreamina and Google Flow free quotas, EU availability) remains unverified.
- Higgsfield currently offers this account a 3-day trial at 0 EUR with unlimited generations on Seedance 2.5 (up to 720p, up to 30 s), Seedance 2.0 (up to 720p, up to 15 s), several image models and text-to-speech. It requires a card and auto-renews to Ultra at 75 EUR for the first month, then 129 EUR/month, unless cancelled within the 3 days. It does not cover Intone, MiniMax H3, Kling 4.0 or Wan 3.0. For a very small budget this is the only verified zero-cost way to run real Seedance tests in French, with a real charge risk if cancellation is missed. Source: Higgsfield live pricing config, 2026-11-25.
- Higgsfield has its own speech tools, so the French voice track can be made on the same platform: Eleven v4 (dialogue with up to 10 voices), Qwen Audio 3.0 TTS Flash (explicit 'fr' language hint, cloned voices), Seed Audio 1.0 and Text to Speech V2, plus cloned 'voice elements' that can be reused for every line. Quoted cost for one short French sentence: 0.03 credits (Qwen), 0.3 (Text to Speech V2, ElevenLabs engine), 0.4 (Seed Audio), 0.46 (Eleven v4). A whole sketch's dialogue costs well under 5 credits, and a reused voice element is a firmer answer to voice consistency than hoping a video model keeps the voice.
- The recommendation's central premise is unproven: nothing verified shows that a Seedance audio reference makes the character speak the supplied recording. The catalogue treats audio references as reference material, separate from audio generation. The only routes where the supplied recording is certainly what is heard are Intone (speech supplied) and Sync Lipsync 3 (lips re-synced afterwards), and neither could be priced.
- Step-up costs within Seedance are not what the recommendation implies. Seedance 2.0 'std' at 480p quotes 15 credits per 5 s, the same as Seedance 2.5 at 480p, so there is no reason to pick 2.0 std. The real ladder per 5 s at 480p is Mini 2.5, Seedance 2.0 fast 5, Seedance 2.5 Flash 10, Seedance 2.5 15. Turning audio off does not lower the Mini price, and the Seedance 2.5 'draft' option costs the same as a normal 480p clip.
- On Higgsfield, only some models accept a voice or audio reference: the four Seedance models, MiniMax H3 and H3 Max, Wan 3.0 and Wan 3.0 Prime (reference mode only), Wan 2.7, MAGI-2, Cinema Studio 4.0 and Grok Video 1.5 (one clip). Kling 4.0, Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash, Veo 3.1, HappyHorse and Grok 1.5 Lite accept none, so voice consistency on those depends entirely on the model.
- Wan 3.0 and MAGI-2 cannot combine a start image with reference media. A shot that needs both an exact first frame and a voice reference is therefore not possible on them; Seedance and MiniMax H3 allow both together.
- On Seedance 2.5 and 2.5 Flash the start and end image work only in 'omni_reference' mode; the default mode is text-to-video.
- MiniMax H3 Max is not the cheap variant at its better resolution: 768p quotes 12.5 credits per 5 s, more than full H3 at 2K (10). Only H3 Max at 480p (7.5) is cheaper.
- Wan 3.0 Prime exists on Higgsfield (same inputs as Wan 3.0; 6.25 credits per 5 s at 480p) and was not assessed. Cinema Studio 4.0, Higgsfield's own model, has the same inputs and price as Seedance 2.5 at 480p (15 credits per 5 s) and was also not assessed.
- Intone requires each character image to show 'one person with a readable front face' and adds 2.5 s to the total length of the voice recordings (output 4-30 s). Glasses and stylised faces are untested.
- From memory only, not a source consulted today: French-language Veo 3 clips were widely made in France during 2025, so 'French not officially evaluated' for Veo does not by itself mean poor French. This is worth one free test if the Google Flow free tier is confirmed.
