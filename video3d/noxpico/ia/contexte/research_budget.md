# Research: budget

## Summary
Research limits first: page fetching was blocked for every site tried (higgsfield.ai, heygen.com and krea.ai are outside this session's allowed network domains) and the shared web-search budget ran out after about 20 queries. Higgsfield figures below are therefore live read-only quotes from the Higgsfield connector on 2026-11-25 (no credits spent, nothing generated); competitor free tiers come from search-result summaries only and often conflict.

Higgsfield: the free plan has no recurring credits (the account holds 10), output is watermarked and reports say Seedance is not open to free accounts. Paid: Plus 1,000 credits at 35 EUR promo (49 list), Ultra 3,000 credits at 75 EUR promo (129 list), VAT extra; a Starter tier (about 19 USD, 270 credits) is reported by third parties but not shown by the connector. The cheapest talking-video model is Seedance 2.0 Mini: 2.5 credits per 5 s clip at 480p with audio, 5 credits at 720p. Mid models cost 10-12.5 credits per clip, Seedance 2.5 costs 35.

One 30 s sketch therefore costs about 60-95 credits on Mini 480p (100-170 at 720p), a 60 s sketch 100-170 (180-320 at 720p); both fit in one Starter or Plus month. A mid-quality model needs 240-415 and 440-790 credits, so a Plus month. A 3-day 0 EUR trial with unlimited Seedance 2.5 exists, but needs a card and auto-charges 75 EUR if not cancelled.

Free elsewhere: Google Flow (reported 50 credits/day, about 5 Veo 3.1 Lite clips) and Dreamina (120 credits/day in the US, about 4 Mini clips) are the only tiers that could plausibly carry a sketch, over 4-12 days, with watermarks. Sora is shut down; Grok's free video is unreliable.

- **A 3-day free trial exists and this account is flagged eligible: 0 EUR now, unlimited generations on top models, card required, then automatic charge.** [high]
  Trial unlimited list: Seedance 2.5 (max 720p, max 30 s per clip), Seedance 2.0 (max 720p, max 15 s), Nano Banana Pro and Nano Banana 2 (up to 2K), GPT Image 2/2.5/2.6 (2K, low/medium), Seedream 5 Pro, voices (Eleven v3, Minimax Speech 2.8 HD, VibeVoice, Seed Speech), Seed Audio 1.0; 8 concurrent jobs; 0 trial credits. ## Recommendation
Plan on Higgsfield with Seedance 2.0 Mini at 480p as the working model: it is the cheapest option with native speech (2.5 credits per 5 s clip), takes a start image plus reference images, outputs 4:3 natively, and the low resolution suits the PS2 look. Budget about 80 credits for a 30 s sketch and about 135 for a 60 s one, stills included.

To pay for it, in order of cost:
1. The 3-day 0 EUR trial (unlimited Seedance 2.5 at 720p, Nano Banana Pro, GPT Image, voices). It can cover both sketches for nothing if script, storyboard and prompts are ready beforehand, but it needs a card and charges 75 EUR + VAT if not cancelled in time. This is the user's decision to make; nothing was started.
2. A Starter month if the pricing page really offers it (reported about 19 USD for 270 credits, Mini and Fast included) - check higgsfield.ai/pricing, because the connector only shows Plus and Ultra.
3. A Plus month at 35 EUR promo (42 EUR with VAT, 1,000 credits), which also allows a mid-quality model (Kling 3.0 or Seedance 2.0 Fast) for one 60 s and one 30 s sketch. The promo is flagged as ending Nov 26.

Use free tiers (Google Flow, Dreamina) only for style tests: they are watermarked, slow (4-12 days per sketch) and their allowances in France are unconfirmed. Before paying, confirm three things on the live pages: the Starter tier, whether the 10 free credits can run any video model, and Dreamina's and Flow's free allowance for a French account.
