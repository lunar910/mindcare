## Missed facts

That is one small look-and-voice test, not a sketch.
- Live Higgsfield price ladder for dialogue-capable video, credits per second (quotes of 2026-11-25): Seedance 2.0 Mini 480p 0.5 (720p 1.0); Wan 3.0 480p 0.75; Seedance 2.0 fast 480p 1.0; Grok Imagine Video 1.5 Lite 480p 1.0; MiniMax H3 Max 480p 1.5; Veo 3.1 Lite with audio 1.5 (no 4:3); Kling 3.0 std 2.0 (no 4:3); Seedance 2.5 Flash 480p 2.0; Grok Video 1.5 480p 2.5; Seedance 2.0 std 480p 3.0; Seedance 2.5 480p 3.0 and 720p 7.0. Seedance 2.0 Mini at 480p is the cheapest by a wide margin.
- Higgsfield has a dedicated dialogue model the researcher did not mention, 'Higgsfield Intone': one or two character images plus an optional location image, driven by one to six uploaded voice recordings (2-30 s in total), output 4-30 s, 480p to 1080p, 4:3 available, up to five shots. Because the voices are supplied as audio files, it removes both the changing-voice problem and the French-accent risk. Its price could not be quoted without uploading audio.
- Higgsfield also offers the pieces for a fixed-voice pipeline: text-to-speech models with preset or cloned voices (Eleven v4 with up to 10 voices in one dialogue, Qwen Audio 3.0 TTS with an explicit 'fr' language option, Text to Speech V2, Seed Audio 1.0), a voice-creation tool, 'Sync Lipsync 3' (re-sync an existing clip's lips to a new audio track), a voice-change tool and a dubbing tool with French as a target. Generating each French line once with one fixed voice per character, then attaching it as audio reference or lip-syncing to it, is a more controllable route than hoping the video model keeps a voice.
- A 3-day trial is offered to this account: 0 EUR today, card required, unlimited Seedance 2.0 (up to 720p, 15 s) and Seedance 2.5 (up to 720p, 30 s), Nano Banana 2/2.1/Pro, GPT Image and text-to-speech for 3 days. Unless cancelled in time it charges 75 EUR for the first month of Ultra and 129 EUR per month afterwards. It could cover a whole sketch for free, but it is a sign-up with a real billing risk, so it is the user's decision.
- The current Higgsfield discounts (Plus 35 EUR monthly or 25 EUR per month annual; Ultra 75 or 45) are a Black Friday offer marked 'Buy until: Nov 26' (2026); list prices are 49 and 129 EUR per month, excluding VAT. Plus gives 1,000 credits, about 13 complete sketches at 75 credits each.
- Seedance via Higgsfield is reachable from the user's own account (the feed prices this account in EUR and lists all Seedance models), so the open question about Dreamina's availability in France does not block the plan.
- Seedance 2.5 Flash exists as a cheaper 2.5 variant (480p/720p, 4-30 s, 2 credits/s at 480p), and Seedance 2.5 has a 480p 'draft' mode that can be finalized at higher resolution within seven days. Seedance 2.0 and 2.0 Mini on Higgsfield also expose a 'genre' hint that includes 'comedy'.
- No ready-made 'PS2' template exists in Higgsfield's Viral or Marketing Studio preset galleries (a search for 'PS2' returned zero results on 2026-11-25), so the look has to come from the prompt and the reference stills.
- Two alternative production routes exist that the 'pure image-to-video' conclusion does not rule out: video-to-video restyling (Higgsfield's Genjutsu Styles include looks named 'Retro Game' and 'Low-Poly' that redraw the user's own filmed video while keeping motion, camera and framing) and motion transfer from a filmed performance onto character images (Genjutsu, Kling 3.0 Motion Control). Very fluid motion and expressive faces are exactly what these routes produce, so some trend videos may be acted on a phone and restyled. I did not view the style previews, so whether 'Retro Game' matches the PS2 cutscene look is unverified.
- Editing does not require CapCut: the Higgsfield connector advertises a 'video-montage' workflow for clip assembly, soundtrack changes and burned-in speech captions, and a 'character-sheet' workflow for consistent multi-view character references.
- The pricing feed contains an entry named 'freegen_card_bonus' with 40 credits expiring after 90 days; its conditions are not stated in the data, so do not count on it without checking the site.

## Notes
Method and limits. I could run no web search (the run's shared search budget was already exhausted when I started) and page fetches failed for higgsfield.ai, docs.byteplus.com and www.flashloop.ai because those hosts are outside the session's allowed network; I stopped fetching after three identical failures, as instructed by the tool. A public GitHub repository cited by the researcher was also not accessible. So I have no independent web sources, and no URLs to give beyond the one the pricing feed itself references.

What I could verify independently is everything about Higgsfield, through the official Higgsfield connector on 2026-11-25: the account balance (free plan, 10 credits), the full video, image and audio model catalogs (durations, resolutions, aspect ratios, reference limits), live cost quotes, the plan pricing feed and the preset galleries. All calls were read-only: nothing was generated, bought or signed up for, and no checkout link was opened. Statements marked 'from memory' come from my training data (cutoff June 2026) and were not re-verified.

Bottom line for the decision. (1) The capability claims that matter are solid: Seedance 2.0 and 2.0 Mini do 4:3 at 480p, 4-15 s, with native dialogue audio, up to 9 reference images and up to 3 voice references; Seedance 2.5 goes to 30 s. (2) The budget section was the weakest part and is corrected: Seedance 2.0 Mini at 480p costs half of what was claimed (4 credits per 8 s), but the cheapest plan actually offered to this account is Plus at 35 EUR for 1,000 credits, not a 9-19 USD tier, and whether the free plan can render video at all is still untested. (3) The claim that 4:3 points to Seedance or Grok is wrong; 4:3 only excludes Kling, Veo and Gemini Omni. (4) Everything about who started the trend, which tool the named accounts use, the tutorials and the exact prompts remains unverified by anyone who actually opened a page; the Seedance attribution is a reasonable inference, not a finding. To raise confidence, the hosts youtube.com, tiktok.com, higgsfield.ai, docs.byteplus.com, flashloop.ai, atlascloud.ai and makeviral.ai would need to be allowed in the session's network settings, or the user can open the two YouTube tutorials directly.
