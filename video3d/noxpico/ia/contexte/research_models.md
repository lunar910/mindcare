# Research: models

## Summary
The field changed a lot in 2026. Sora 2 is gone (app closed 26 April 2026, API shut 24 September 2026). The current leaders for "start image + spoken line + lip-sync" are ByteDance Seedance 2.5 (31 July 2026; 2.0 and 2.0 Mini still sold), Kling 4.0 (October 2026), Google Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash (5 November 2026, alongside Veo 3.1), Alibaba Wan 3.0 (24 August 2026), MiniMax H3 (31 July 2026) and xAI Grok Imagine Video 1.5 (June 2026).

For this sketch, the Seedance family fits best. It takes a start image plus many reference images, takes audio references for the voice, outputs 4:3 and 9:16, runs up to 15 s (2.0) or 30 s (2.5), and currently tops the image-to-video-with-audio and lip-sync arenas. It refuses real human faces as references, and 3D characters are the accepted route.

No independent French benchmark exists for any model. French is officially listed only for Kling 4.0, MiniMax H3 and HappyHorse. For Seedance, Wan, Omni and Grok it is claimed by third parties, and Google says only English is fully evaluated. The safest route for French and for a stable voice is to supply the French voice track yourself as an audio reference.

All of these models are in the Higgsfield catalogue of the existing account (free plan, 10 credits, checked today). Quotes for 5 s: Seedance 2.0 Mini 480p 2.5 credits, Wan 3.0 480p 3.75, Kling 4.0 Flash 7.5, MiniMax H3 10, Seedance 2.5 480p 15. Whether the free plan can actually run a video model is unconfirmed.

Page fetching was blocked in this session (pinggy.io, dreamina.capcut.com and genra.ai would need to be allowed), so web facts come from search extracts, not full-page reads.

## Findings
- **Sora 2 is no longer available and should be dropped from the comparison.** [high]
  OpenAI announced the shutdown on 24 March 2026. The consumer app (web and mobile) closed on 26 April 2026. The Videos API and the sora-2 / sora-2-pro models were removed on 24 September 2026, with no replacement model and no video feature in ChatGPT. One source notes Azure still listed Sora 2 until 15 October 2026. It is absent from the Higgsfield catalogue checked today.
- **Seedance 2.5 / 2.0 / 2.0 Mini (ByteDance) is the best technical fit: start image, many references, audio references, 4:3 and 9:16, long clips, top arena rank.** [high]
  From the Higgsfield catalogue (read today): Seedance 2.5 and 2.5 Flash run 4-30 s, Seedance 2.0 and 2.0 Mini run 4-15 s. All accept start_image and end_image, with native audio on by default. References: 2.0 and Mini take up to 9 images, 3 videos (2-15 s) and 3 audio clips (2-15 s); 2.5 takes up to 30 images, 10 videos and 10 audio clips; 2.5 Flash takes 10 / 5 / 5. Aspect ratios: auto, 21:9, 16:9, 4:3, 1:1, 3:4, 9:16. Resolutions: Mini and Flash 480p/720p; 2.0 and 2.5 up to 4K. The 50-reference limit and 30 s length match ByteDance's press release (launch 31 July 2026, global 3 August 2026). The most recent Artificial Analysis image-to-video-with-audio board seen puts Seedance 2.5 first (Elo 1161) and Seedance 2.0 at 1141; earlier snapshots had Seedance 2.0, MiniMax H3 and Gemini Omni Flash within about 15 Elo of each other. A crowd lip-sync arena (Oakgen) ranks Seedance 2.5 first (about 1123), Seedance 2.0 about 1060, Wan 3.0 about 923. Reviews say Mini is acceptable for low-movement conversation but standard 2.0 has more precise lip-sync.
- **Seedance French support is claimed but not officially confirmed, and its voice consistency relies on audio references whose behaviour is disputed.** [medium]
  The official Dreamina page for Seedance 2.5 reportedly lists 11 speech languages without French (Chinese, English, Spanish, Indonesian, Malay, Thai, Arabic, Portuguese, Vietnamese, Japanese, Korean). Several third-party guides list French for 2.0 (7-8 languages) and 2.5, and one guide says official demos include French dialogue. Guides advise naming the language explicitly in the prompt, otherwise the model may treat the scene as English. Voice across shots: an OpenArt tutorial reports that a 15 s voice clip tagged as @Audio1 was reused across videos, and a Genflick test found audio references preserve speaker identity; two other sites say it is a style guide, not cloning. Voice drifts between generations without a reference. Two characters in one shot work (one review describes 30 s two-person scenes with stable faces), but wrong-speaker errors occur; guides recommend one speaking character per beat.
- **Seedance 2.x refuses reference images or videos containing real human faces; 3D or illustrated characters are the accepted route.** [medium]
  Blogs quoting ByteDance ModelArk documentation say Seedance 2.5 and 2.0 do not support uploading references with real human faces; Dreamina shows 'Real human faces are not supported' and Adobe Firefly applies the same rule. Generated or illustrated 3D characters pass. This suits game-cutscene characters, but a very photorealistic render could trigger the detector; no source tests that case, and no Higgsfield-specific policy was found.
- **Kling 4.0 officially lists French and has the clearest character-plus-voice binding, but it offers no 4:3 on Higgsfield and costs more per second.** [medium]
  Kling 4.0 launched globally in October 2026 (available to all accounts from 15 October per one source). Catalogue: 3-30 s (Flash 3-20 s, 720p only), start and end image, up to 10 keyframes or reference images, up to 7 elements, audio always on. Aspect ratios on Higgsfield: 21:9, 16:9, 9:16, 1:1, auto, so 4:3 must be cropped. Kling's release notes list dialogue in Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean, Spanish, Portuguese, German, French, Hindi and more. Kling 3.0, by contrast, officially covers only English, Chinese, Japanese, Korean and Spanish, with French described as partial. Voice: a voice can be bound to a character element (up to 3 elements carry a custom voice, from a 5-30 s sample on Omni); Kling warns that a standalone voice reference is not automatically tied to a character. Multi-character dialogue is supported. Prices: Kling 4.0 API about $0.157/s at 720p; Kling's own app reported at 10 credits/s at 720p (other sources say the rate is unpublished); paid plans from $6.99-10 per month (660 credits); free tier 66 credits/day where the account is eligible.
- **Google now has two relevant models: Veo 3.1 (4/6/8 s) and the newer Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash (3-15 s, $0.07/s); neither officially evaluates French and neither outputs 4:3.** [medium]
  Veo 3.1: clips of 4, 6 or 8 s, start image, up to 3 reference images (reference mode is 8 s only), 16:9 and 9:16 only, native audio, no voice reference. Reviewers quoting Google's docs say English is the only fully evaluated language and other languages vary; the model sometimes paraphrases the quoted line. No Veo 4 release is confirmed. API prices: Lite about $0.05/s at 720p with audio, Fast $0.10/s, Standard $0.40/s. Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash (announced 5 November 2026): 3-15 s from text or a single first frame, 3-10 s with references or first/last frames, up to 7-10 reference images and 3 reference videos, native dialogue, 16:9 and 9:16 only on Higgsfield, $0.07/s at 720p (30% cheaper than 1.1). Voice options follow Gemini TTS (30 preset voices, Voice Design, Voice Replication); one blog reports distinct lip-synced voices for several characters from short voice references. A third-party site lists French among about 11 speech languages; a GitHub guide says only English is fully evaluated. Flow gives every Google account 50 credits/day (about five 8 s Veo 3.1 Lite clips at 10 credits each, or roughly two 720p Omni clips), with a 10 s cap and possible peak-hour blocking for free users. Generation works in the EEA; uploading your own video for editing is blocked there. One guide says Veo 3.1 Fast, Veo 3.1 Quality and Omni 1.1 retire from Flow on 5 December 2026.
- **MiniMax H3 (Hailuo 3.0) is the one model whose official model card lists French among its stable dialogue languages.** [medium]
  Released 31 July 2026 with open weights. The Hugging Face model card lists 11 stable languages: Arabic, Chinese, English, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, Russian, Spanish. Catalogue: 4-15 s, native 2K, start and end image, up to 9 reference images, 3 videos and 3 audio clips, aspect ratios including 4:3 and 9:16; H3 Max is a faster 480p/768p variant. Audio is 32 kHz stereo generated in the same pass. Earlier Artificial Analysis snapshots placed H3 and H3 Max in the top three for image-to-video with audio. API price about $0.08/s at 768p, up to about $0.13-0.14/s at 2K.
- **Wan 3.0 (Alibaba) is the cheapest long-clip option with 4:3, but its lip-sync ranks below Seedance and French is only claimed by third parties.** [medium]
  Launched 24 August 2026, no open weights. Catalogue: 2-30 s, 480p/720p/1080p, native audio, start and end image, or reference mode with up to 10 images, 5 videos and 5 audio clips; a start image cannot be combined with reference media. Aspect ratios: 16:9, 9:16, 1:1, 4:3, 3:4. Alibaba list price (Singapore): $0.05/s at 480p, $0.10/s at 720p, $0.20/s at 1080p. Third-party guides claim phoneme-level lip-sync in 12 languages including French; another review says no official language count exists. The Oakgen lip-sync arena scores it about 923 against about 1123 for Seedance 2.5, with weaker multi-character and talking sub-scores.
- **Grok Imagine Video 1.5 has voice references and many image references, but French quality and free access are unverified.** [medium]
  Official xAI docs: lip-synced speech, text-to-video and image-to-video, up to 14 reference images and 3 voice references, first/mid/last frame pinning, clips up to 15 s. Sources disagree on whether the voice references are presets or uploads, and whether they are generally available or on request. Price: $0.08/s at 480p, $0.14/s at 720p, $0.25/s at 1080p; the Lite variant starts at about $0.02-0.03/s and supports 4:3 on Higgsfield. A news article says a language such as French can be named in the prompt; one review says even English lip-sync needs checking. Reports on the free consumer tier conflict (from none to about 5 videos a day). No EU-specific information was found.
- **Vidu, PixVerse, Runway, Luma and HappyHorse are weaker choices for French dialogue.** [medium]
  Vidu Q3 Pro: native audio and multi-speaker dialogue up to 16 s, but only English, Japanese and Chinese are listed; about $0.10/s at 720p. PixVerse V6: native speech, 15 s at 1080p, multi-image character references, about $0.035/s at 360p; no French information found. Runway Gen-4.5: audio added in May 2026 through an ElevenLabs integration, described as general facial movement rather than precise lip-sync; a November 2026 comparison says its API has no audio parameter. Luma Ray 3.2: native audio is unconfirmed. HappyHorse 1.0/1.1 (Alibaba, 1.1 released 22 June 2026): 7 lip-sync languages including French, 3-15 s, up to 9 reference images, but only a start frame on Higgsfield and reviewers flag imprecise audio sync. Two newer entrants with audio, MAGI-2 (Sand AI, 5 August 2026, open weights) and FLUX 3 Video (Black Forest Labs, 23 July 2026), have no documented speech languages.
- **Higgsfield credit quotes for a 5 s clip with audio, taken today on the existing account, show a 14-fold price spread between models.** [high]
  Balance: 10 credits, free plan. Quotes (credits per 5 s unless stated): Seedance 2.0 Mini 480p 2.5 (720p 5; 10 s at 480p 5); Wan 3.0 480p 3.75 (720p 7.5); Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash 360p 3.75 (720p 11.25); Seedance 2.0 fast 480p 5; Grok 1.5 Lite 480p 5; Kling 3.0 Omni std 6.25; Kling 4.0 Flash 720p 7.5; Wan 2.7 720p 7.5; MiniMax H3 Max 480p 7.5; Seedance 2.5 Flash 480p 10 (720p 25); MiniMax H3 2K 10; Kling 3.0 std 10; Kling 2.6 10; Kling 4.0 720p 12.5; Grok 1.5 480p 12.5; HappyHorse 720p 12.5; MAGI-2 1080p 12.5; Seedance 2.5 480p 15 (720p 35; 15 s at 480p 45); FLUX 3 Video 720p 30. For 8 s: Veo 3.1 Lite with audio 12, Veo 3.1 Fast 32. A quote does not check plan access. The catalogue itself says Seedance 2.0 'std' needs Plus and 'fast' needs Starter, and web sources say Starter excludes Seedance 2.5.
- **Higgsfield plan prices put the cheapest usable route at roughly $0.035-0.05 per second, and the free plan probably cannot generate video.** [medium]
  Most-cited prices: Starter $19/month for 270 credits (about $0.070 per credit), Plus $59/month or $47/month billed annually for 1,200 credits (about $0.049 or $0.039 per credit), Ultra $129/month for 3,000. Sources conflict on November figures (a 12 November review reports a sale at $15 for 200 and $39 for 1,000). At the Starter rate: Seedance 2.0 Mini 480p is about $0.035/s, Wan 3.0 480p about $0.053/s, Kling 4.0 Flash about $0.106/s, Seedance 2.5 480p about $0.21/s and 720p about $0.49/s. Higgsfield's pricing page describes the free tier as limited models, no monthly credits, one job at a time; third-party guides say it has no video and that the 10 welcome credits are too few for a Seedance 2.0 video. Free output may carry a watermark.
- **Higgsfield also offers audio-driven tools that sidestep the French and voice-consistency problem entirely.** [medium]
  Catalogue entries: Intone, a talking-head model where one or two characters (up to 2 character images, optional location image) speak one to six supplied voice recordings totalling 2-30 s, alone or to each other, at 480p-1080p in ratios including 4:3 and 9:16; a third-party test reports 21 credits per scene at 480p. Sync Lipsync 3 re-syncs lips of an existing video to a supplied audio track. A dubbing tool lists French ('fra') as a target, and a voice-change tool accepts saved voice elements. Because the speech is supplied, language and voice are fixed by the recording, not by the video model.
- **Seedance availability in France: on Dreamina since spring 2026 for 2.0, with 2.5 rolling out to European subscribers; free daily credits there cover only a few seconds.** [medium]
  CapCut's newsroom says Dreamina Seedance 2.0 expanded to more markets including Europe on 28 March 2026. Dreamina's 2.5 launch page says rollout to subscriber accounts of people over 16 across Europe (two versions of the page give 31 July and 24 October 2026). Higgsfield's own blog calls Seedance availability 'patchy across Europe'. Dreamina free accounts in the US reportedly get 120 credits/day while 720p Seedance 2.5 costs 37 credits per second, so about 3 s a day; other regions get different allowances and no France figure was found. BytePlus lists Seedance 2.5 at $0.231/s at 720p; the launch offer was from $0.097/s.
- **No source identifies which model the viral 'PS2/PS3 cutscene' skit accounts use; guides for that look point to image-first workflows with Seedance, Kling 4.0, Omni or MiniMax.** [low]
  An Atlas Cloud guide for a PS2-era cutscene look locks a low-poly portrait as the first frame and animates it with Omni 1.1 Flash, Kling 4.0 or MiniMax. A DEV Community 'still-first' workflow recommends Seedance 2.5 for people, bodies, hands and two people in frame, with clips of about 5-8 s instead of one long generation. A reviewer who tested 50 clips recommends a timecoded shot list and short spoken lines for clean lip-sync. These are vendor or creator blogs, not independent tests.

## Recommendation
Ranked shortlist for a stylised game-cutscene sketch with French dialogue, stable face and voice, on a small budget:

1. Seedance on Higgsfield, with the French voice supplied as an audio reference. Draft with Seedance 2.0 Mini at 480p (0.5 credit/s, about $0.035/s at the Starter rate); use Seedance 2.0 or 2.5 only for shots where Mini's lip-sync fails (2.5 costs 3 credits/s at 480p and reportedly needs the Plus plan). It is the only family combining start image, many character references, audio references, native 4:3 and top lip-sync rank. Supplying the voice track removes the two main risks (French not on the official 2.5 language list, voice drift between shots). 480p suits the low-resolution look.
2. MiniMax H3 (2 credits/s at 2K): the fallback if native French generation is preferred, since French is on its official language list; it also takes audio references and 4:3.
3. Kling 4.0 Flash (1.5 credits/s): French is officially listed and a voice can be bound to a character element, but there is no 4:3 (crop from 16:9) and audio is always on.
4. Wan 3.0 (0.75 credit/s at 480p): cheapest for long takes with 4:3, but weaker lip-sync and unverified French.
5. Google Flow free tier (50 credits/day, Veo 3.1 Lite or Omni 1.2 Flash): the only clearly free route from France, useful for tests; no 4:3, French not officially evaluated.
6. Grok Imagine 1.5: only if its voice references prove usable in French.

Skip Sora 2 (shut down), Vidu Q3 (no French), Runway and Luma (no reliable native lip-sync), PixVerse and HappyHorse (unverified or weaker).

Budget guide: a 45 s sketch cut into nine 5 s shots costs about 22.5 credits per pass on Seedance 2.0 Mini 480p; with three takes per shot, about 68 credits, roughly a quarter of one $19 Starter month. The same on Seedance 2.5 at 480p is 135 credits per pass.

Before paying, run one identical test (same start image, same one-line French sentence, 5 s, 4:3) on Seedance 2.0 Mini, MiniMax H3 and Kling 4.0 Flash, since no published French benchmark exists. The 10 free credits would cover that only if the free plan allows video, which is unconfirmed. Higgsfield Intone is worth one test too, because it is built for two characters speaking supplied recordings.
