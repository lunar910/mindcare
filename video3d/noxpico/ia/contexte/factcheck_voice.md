# Fact-check of research: voice

- [uncertain] 1. Veo 3.1 native voices drift between generations; Flow 'Voice ingredients' (Veo 3.1 Fast, Ultra plan) and preset 'custom voices' in Veo 3.1 Lite exist; no way to upload your own voice sample to Veo.
  correction: Not re-verified on the web (see notes: no web access in this run). Only independent evidence: in the Higgsfield catalog read today, Veo 3.1 accepts a start image only and Veo 3.1 Lite a start and end image, with no audio or voice input; clips are 4, 6 or 8 s. That fits 'no voice lock on Veo' for this user's platform. The Flow voice features and their plan/region limits remain unchecked.
- [confirmed] 2a. Seedance input limits: 2.0 takes up to 3 audio clips of 2-15 s; 2.5 takes up to 10 reference audio files, paired with an image or video reference, clips up to 30 s.
- [uncertain] 2b. Seedance 2.5 audio references are limited to 30 s combined.
  correction: Not stated in the Higgsfield catalog (it only says max 10 audio references); could not be checked elsewhere.
- [uncertain] 2c. With a Seedance audio reference the output voice is guided, not copied; Seedance 2.0 had the best lip-sync among prompt-to-speech models in a Curious Refuge comparison.
  correction: Could not be checked. One indirect hint: Higgsfield's own parameter text says generate_audio produces 'native audio ... Independent of audio_references', which fits 'the model generates a new track' but proves nothing about how close the voice lands. This is still the main unknown of the recommended route; only a real test settles it.
- [uncertain] 3a. French in Seedance is claimed by vendors but not independently tested; there is no language setting, so French must be requested in the prompt.
  correction: The 'no language setting' part is confirmed on Higgsfield: none of the four Seedance entries has a language parameter. The quality of Seedance French and the reported desync limits (5 words, 10 s) could not be checked.
- [uncertain] 3b. Kling 3.0 / 3.0 Omni native audio lists Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean and Spanish only (no French); voice binding uses a 5-30 s sample.

The researcher did not cover Kling 4.0 and 4.0 Flash, which are now in the catalog; their audio and French support is unknown.

## Missed facts
- Wan 3.0 is a second budget candidate the researcher did not list: audio references (up to 5), native audio, clips 2-30 s, 4:3 supported, 480p at 0.75 credit/s (7.5 credits per 10 s, 3 credits for a 4 s test); Wan 3.0 Prime is 12.5 credits per 10 s at 480p. Its French quality is unknown.
- Kling 4.0 (25 credits per 10 s at 720p, clips up to 30 s) and Kling 4.0 Flash (15 credits per 10 s, up to 20 s) are in the Higgsfield catalog; the pricing data carries a Kling 4 release promotion dated 21 Oct 2026. The research only assessed Kling 3.0, so 'avoid Kling for French' is not established for the current version.
- Higgsfield Change Voice costs 0.1 credit per second (3 credits per 30 s), so the in-platform voice-lock step is cheap. The 120+ preset voices carry only a name and gender, no language or accent field, so a French-sounding preset can only be found by listening to previews; a specific French voice needs a custom voice element.
- Buying credits: the live pricing response for this free account shows only Plus (1,000 credits/month; 49 EUR list, 35 EUR monthly or 25 EUR/month annual in a sale ending 26 Nov 2026) and Ultra (3,000 credits; 129 EUR list). No one-time top-up packs are offered to it, and top-up credits expire after 90 days. Prices exclude VAT.
- Higgsfield currently offers this account a 3-day trial at 0 EUR with unlimited Seedance 2.5 (up to 720p, 30 s), Seedance 2.0 (up to 720p, 15 s), Seed Audio 1.0 and the TTS engines. It requires a card and auto-charges 75 EUR, then 129 EUR/month, unless cancelled before the 3 days end. This is a decision for the user, not something to start on his behalf.
- Sync Lipsync 3 is available on Higgsfield but costs 3.6 credits per second (108 credits per 30 s), which rules out 'generate then re-sync' on a small budget there.
- Higgsfield describes Intone itself as 'Talking-head video' and requires character images showing 'one person with a readable front face'; its default output is 1080p at high bitrate and its price cannot be quoted without uploading audio. Treat its full-body acting as unproven.
- Plain Seedance 2.0 in 'std' mode costs 3 credits per second even at 480p; only 'Mini' (0.5) and 'fast' (1.0) are cheap. Seedance 2.5 Flash accepts 5 audio references, not 10, and tops out at 720p.
- Higgsfield has first-party talking-character pipelines (drama-ads, short-drama, and a character-sheet workflow with 'Classic 3D' and 'CGI' styles). They were not examined because they load only on an explicit command, but they target exactly 'characters act and talk on screen'.
- From memory only, not checked today: in early 2026 several Seedance 2.0 platforms restricted realistic human faces as reference images. A 'realistic PS2/PS3' character that reads as a real person might be affected, so the first test should use the actual character image.
- Higgsfield has a dubbing model with French ('fra') as a target, but 'generate in English, dub to French' would need a lip re-sync at 3.6 credits per second, so it is not a budget route.

The stated ways to pay beyond the 10 free credits need revising (no small top-up on offer; Plus at 35-49 EUR is the entry), and Wan 3.0, Kling 4.0 and the 0.1 credit/s Change Voice price should be added to the comparison.
